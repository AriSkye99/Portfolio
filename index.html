About Me

I'm Ari, an aspiring developer with a passion for learning, creating, and solving problems. Whether it's designing a website, experimenting with game development, or tackling a new programming concept, I enjoy the process of turning ideas into something real. Programming has become the perfect outlet for that curiosity, combining creativity, logic, and problem-solving in a way that keeps me excited to learn every day.

I'm currently expanding my knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript through self-directed learning and hands-on projects, while also exploring game development with Unity. Rather than focusing only on tutorials, I enjoy applying what I learn by creating projects that challenge me to think, experiment, and improve. Every project I build helps strengthen my technical skills while teaching me new approaches to designing, developing, and improving software. My goal isn't just to learn programming languages—it's to become someone who can create meaningful, well-designed experiences through technology. I believe the best way to learn is by building, making mistakes, and continually improving.

This portfolio is a reflection of that journey. As I continue learning and gaining experience, I'll be adding new projects, refining old ones, and documenting my growth as a developer.

“With enough time and determination, there’s nothing I can’t learn or figure out.” -Ari
